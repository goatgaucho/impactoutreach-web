# ImpactOutreach: Competitive Landscape

Reference document mapping competitors, their capabilities, pricing, and how our features align.

---

## Direct Competitors

### AdvocacyAI
**The closest competitor to what we do.**

- **What they do:** AI-native advocacy platform. AI personas generate unique messages based on advocate type (doctor, donor, CEO). AI-powered A/B testing adjusts subject lines, body copy, send times, and channels in real time. Zero-code campaign builder launches campaigns in 5-10 minutes.
- **Channels:** Email, click-to-call, tweets, regulatory comments
- **Data:** Matches supporters to 300M+ voter records in 0.2 seconds
- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed — likely mid-market SaaS
- **Website:** advocacyai.com

**What they have that we don't (yet):**
- Advocate persona system (doctor vs. donor vs. CEO messaging)
- AI-powered A/B testing with real-time optimization
- Regulatory comment routing
- Tweet routing
- Voter record matching
- Zero-code campaign builder

**What we have that they don't:**
- Multi-brand network (15-20 independent-looking organizations)
- Intentional message imperfection (typos, varied polish levels) for authenticity
- Canadian-specific infrastructure

---

### CiviClick
- **What they do:** Advocacy platform with a 58-million-person first-party activist database. AI-powered regulatory comment generation. Advanced gamification. Real-time analytics.
- **Channels:** Email, SMS, regulatory comments
- **Differentiators:** Gamification (points, badges, leaderboards), massive activist database, Salesforce/HubSpot/MailChimp integrations
- **Pricing:** Custom; 501(c)(3) nonprofit discounts available
- **Website:** civiclick.com
- **Launched CiviClick 2.0:** January 2025

**Relevant to us:**
- Their gamification approach is a model for driving repeat engagement
- Their regulatory comment automation is a feature we should add
- Their activist database is a data asset — we could build something similar over time

---

### New/Mode
**The only real Canadian-focused player.**

- **What they do:** Multi-channel advocacy tools covering US, UK, EU, Australia, and Canada. Canadian-founded.
- **Channels:** One-Click Calling, One-Click Email, One-Click Fax, Tweet Storm, Letters to the Editor, Bluesky (added 2026)
- **Pricing:** Free forever tier; paid plans start at $99/month
- **Website:** newmode.net

**Relevant to us:**
- They prove the Canadian market exists and is viable
- Their Letters to the Editor tool is a feature we should replicate
- Their Tweet Storm tool is a model for coordinated social media pressure
- At $99/month self-serve, they're positioned very differently from what we offer — we're a managed, AI-native platform; they're a DIY toolkit
- They added Bluesky support in 2026 — signals where social advocacy is migrating

## Enterprise Platforms

### Quorum (owns Capitol Canary / Phone2Action)
**The dominant enterprise player after acquiring Capitol Canary in 2022.**

- **What they do:** Nine product modules — Federal, State, Local, School Board, Grassroots, PAC, Stakeholder, International, Professional Services. AI assistant "Quincy" analyzes legislation, summarizes hearings, drafts communications, surfaces stakeholder insights.
- **Channels:** Email, click-to-call, video messages (new), social media
- **Key feature:** Video messages — lets supporters record and submit personal video testimonials to legislators. Described as the highest-impact advocacy format for 2026.
- **Pricing:** Tiered (Basic/Pro/Enterprise) — typically $30K-$150K+/year with setup fees
- **Website:** quorum.us

**Relevant to us:**
- Their video testimonial tool is a capability we should consider adding
- Their legislative tracking + grassroots integration is the auto-trigger campaign model
- They're enterprise-priced — we can compete below them on cost while matching key features
- Their scale (nine modules) shows where a full advocacy platform can go

---

### FiscalNote (owns VoterVoice)
**Publicly traded (NYSE: NOTE). The data and intelligence side of advocacy.**

- **What they do:** Legislative tracking (CQ Federal), issue management (PolicyNote), digital advocacy (VoterVoice). SOC-2 Type 2 compliant. Their annual Advocacy Benchmark Report (analyzing 545M+ emails, 23M texts, 17M page views from 2,000+ organizations) is the industry standard.
- **Channels:** Email, SMS (12M+ texts in 2024), click-to-call
- **Pricing:** Enterprise — custom pricing
- **Website:** fiscalnote.com

**Relevant to us — benchmark data from their 2025 report:**
- Average email open rate: 39.2%
- Action rate: 6.9%
- Best-performing: dual federal + state campaigns at 9.5% action rate
- Labor organizations: 45-50% open rates
- Single-issue causes: 13.2% action rate
- Saturday 7 AM is the optimal send time
- These benchmarks are what our client analytics dashboard should measure against

---

### Bonterra (EveryAction)
- **What they do:** Combines grassroots advocacy with donor engagement and multichannel outreach. Their differentiator is connecting advocacy to fundraising — automatically following up action-takers with AI-optimized donation asks.
- **Key claim:** 99.6% deliverability to legislators by prioritizing website comment forms over email
- **Channels:** Email, direct mail, advocacy actions, fundraising
- **Website:** bonterratech.com

**Relevant to us:**
- Their advocacy-to-fundraising pipeline is a model for connecting campaign actions to revenue
- Their deliverability claim (99.6%) via comment form prioritization is a tactic worth investigating
- They're strong in the nonprofit/progressive space — different market segment from what we target

## Specialized / Niche Players

### One Click Politics
- **What they do:** Multi-channel advocacy for 400+ organizations. Integrates with NationBuilder (75+ NB customers use it).
- **Channels:** Video messages to lawmakers, mass email, online petitions, SMS blasts, posting to legislators' Facebook and Twitter, live patch-through calls
- **Website:** Available via NationBuilder marketplace

**Relevant to us:**
- Their patch-through calling is the click-to-call model we could add
- Social media posting to legislators' pages is a capability to replicate
- Petition functionality is table stakes we could consider

---

### Muster
- **What they do:** User-friendly advocacy platform focused on tracking advocate engagement, Action Alerts, and data analysis. Positioned as simpler than Quorum or FiscalNote.
- **Website:** muster.com

**Relevant to us:**
- Their positioning as "simpler" shows there's demand for accessible advocacy tools vs. enterprise complexity

---

### Grassroots Unwired
- **What they do:** Mobile-first canvassing with GPS-enabled data collection, real-time field director dashboards, multilingual scripts, PCI-compliant donation capture.
- **Focus:** Nonprofits, progressive campaigns, labor unions
- **Website:** grassrootsunwired.com

**Relevant to us:**
- Their field canvassing + GPS approach is a different channel (in-person) we haven't considered
- Multilingual support is relevant for Canadian bilingual campaigns

---

### Plural Policy
- **What they do:** AI-powered legislative tracking across all 50 US states + Congress + DC + Puerto Rico. Focused on intelligence/monitoring, not grassroots mobilization.
- **Website:** pluralpolicy.com

**Relevant to us:**
- Their legislative monitoring is the intelligence layer that could feed our auto-trigger campaign system
- They're monitoring-only — no action/mobilization side — so they're a potential integration partner, not a competitor

---

### Grassroots Online (Canada)
- **What they do:** Canada's premiere digital public affairs agency (founded 2009). "Advocacy Concierge" walks supporters through signup from Facebook or websites.
- **Model:** Agency/service model, NOT a SaaS platform
- **Website:** grassrootsonline.ca

**Relevant to us:**
- They prove demand for Canadian advocacy services
- Their agency model means they're not direct SaaS competition
- They could potentially be a customer for a white-label version of our platform

## Our Positioning

**What makes us different:**

1. **Multi-brand architecture** — No one else runs campaigns across 15-20 independent-looking advocacy organizations. Every competitor is a single-org tool.
2. **Authentic AI generation** — Intentional imperfection (varied polish, typos, style variation) designed to pass as genuine constituent communication. Competitors generate clean, polished text that reads as machine-generated.
3. **Canadian focus** — The Canadian advocacy tech market is badly underserved. We're positioned to own it.
4. **Managed platform** — We're not a self-serve tool. We deploy and operate campaigns — clients bring the list and the issues, we run the machine.

**Where we need to catch up:**
- Advocate personas (AdvocacyAI has this)
- A/B testing and real-time optimization (AdvocacyAI has this)
- Regulatory comment routing (AdvocacyAI and CiviClick have this)
- Client-facing analytics (everyone has this)
- Social media channels (New/Mode and One Click Politics have this)

**Where we can leapfrog:**
- Multi-channel + multi-brand is a combination no one offers
- AI calling is not offered by any competitor in this space
- Auto-trigger campaigns (legislative monitoring → automatic campaign launch) doesn't exist as an integrated product
- White-label SaaS for agencies would create a new market category
